## What I do

- **Own the AI architecture** at Konecto: how agents are built, evaluated, deployed and improved, and which models and vendors we use.
- **Design voice-agent systems end to end**: real-time speech pipelines, retrieval over technical knowledge, tool calling and integrations with client systems.
- **Built our own voice-agent platform** to replace a third-party vendor, and the evaluation and deployment tooling around it.
- **Lead the engineering team**: planning, code review, hiring, and the AI-assisted workflow the team builds with.
- **Research and teach**: speech anti-spoofing, computer vision and reinforcement learning, and master's-level NLP courses.

## Research

**MARSA: A Multi-accent, Multi-system Corpus of Full and Partial Voice Spoofing Attacks for Spanish Anti-spoofing Research**<br/>
T. Acosta-Bernal, J.C. Vásquez-Correa, M.D. Calderón-Acosta, R.F. Manrique · manuscript in preparation for *Scientific Data*<br/>
[![Dataset DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/dataset-10.5281%2Fzenodo.23873176-1682d4?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23873176) [![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/code-GitHub-24292f?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Tomas1307/ANTI-SPOOFING-VOICE-LATIN-AMERICA)<br/>
The first large-scale Spanish anti-spoofing corpus: 240,995 utterances, 1,567 speakers, 7 accent regions, 6 open voice-cloning systems, full and word-level partial spoofs.

**Eyes in the Sky: Deep Learning Processing of Airborne Sensor Imagery for Environmental Monitoring in the Colombian Amazon**<br/>
T. Acosta-Bernal et al. · *IEEE TechDefense 2025*, Rome<br/>
[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.1109%2FTECHDEFENSE67306.2025.11721025-00629b?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TECHDEFENSE67306.2025.11721025) [![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/code-GitHub-24292f?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Tomas1307/FLIR_GPF2)<br/>
YOLO detection of illegal mining camps in aerial thermal (FLIR) video: 93.4% mAP@50.

**Multi-Agent Evolution for Electric Vehicle Charging Systems: A Scatter Search Approach to Breeding Specialized DQN Agents**<br/>
T. Acosta Bernal, A. Tabares · in progress<br/>
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/code-GitHub-24292f?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Tomas1307/MultiAgent-Evolution-DQN-EV-Systems)<br/>
Reinforcement learning combined with MILP: five specialized DQN agent types for 592 vehicles, solving instances where MILP alone was infeasible on 35% of test systems.

## Teaching

Instructor at Universidad de los Andes for **Fundamentals of Natural Language Processing** and **Advanced Natural Language Processing** (master's level). For the advanced course I built the final competition benchmark: a 216-document corpus and 1,500 validated questions to evaluate the students' RAG and tool-calling agents.
